Our Mission

The mission of the ECE GSA is to cultivate a welcoming and inclusive environment for graduate students in the Electrical and Computer Engineering industry. Throughout our organization's early stages, we consistently lead the way in arranging industry events, workshops, and networking opportunities to enrich the academic and professional growth of our members.
